# Store Analytics

This module gives you a detailed overview of your store analytics.

## View Analytics
To view store analytics:
1. Navigate to **Store Management > Analytics**.
2. Review summary cards for **Total P/O**, **Total SPR**, **Orders**, and **Stock Value**.
3. Review the **Monthly Purchases** bar chart and **Recent Activity Breakdown** donut chart.
4. Review the **Latest PO** table (Item Code, Department Name, Supplier Name, Amount) and the **Top Suppliers** list.

![Analytics](/img/store/analytics/analytics.png)

## Filter Analytics
To apply filters to your analytics:
1. Click the **Filter** button on the top-right of the Store Analytics page.
2. Set the desired **Date Range**, **Department**, and **Supplier**.
3. Click **Apply** to update the dashboard with the filtered results, or **Reset** to clear all filters.

![Analytics Filter](/img/store/analytics/analytics-filter.png)
