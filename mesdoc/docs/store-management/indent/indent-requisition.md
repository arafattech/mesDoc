# Indent Requisition

Manage internal department requests for items to be purchased.

## Indent Requisition List
To view the Indent Requisition list:
1. Navigate to **Store Management > Indent > Indent Requisition**.
2. Use the **Submitted**, **Approved**, and **Rejected** tabs to filter records by status.
3. The table shows Indent No, Requested by, Department, Request Date, Required Date, GM Status, Store Status, Urgency, Items, and Complete status.

![Indent Requisition List](/img/store/indent/indent-requisition-list.png)

## Add a New Indent Requisition
To create a new indent requisition:
1. Click the **New** button.
2. Select the **For Department**, **Urgency**, and **Required By** date, and add a **Note** if needed.
3. Add items to the **Items to Purchase** table, or click **Associate** to pull items from an existing request.
4. Click **Submit** to save, or **Cancel** to discard.

![Add Indent Requisition](/img/store/indent/indent-requisition-add.png)

## View Indent Requisition Details
To view a specific requisition:
1. Click the **info** icon in the Actions column of a row.
2. Review the Indent No, Department, Urgency, Required By date, Note, and Items to Purchase.

![View Indent Requisition](/img/store/indent/indent-requisition-view.png)
