# Indent Requests

Review and approve or reject indent requisitions raised by other departments.

## Indent Requests List
To view the Indent Requests list:
1. Navigate to **Store Management > Indent > Indent Requests**.
2. Use the **Submitted**, **Approved**, and **Rejected** tabs to filter records by status.

![Indent Requests List](/img/store/indent/indent-requests-list.png)

## Review an Indent Request
To approve or reject a request:
1. Click the **info** icon in the Actions column of a row.
2. Review the Indent No, For Department, Urgency, Required By date, Note, and the Items to Purchase table, adjusting the **Approved Qty** for each item if needed.
3. Click **Approve** to accept the request, **Reject** and add a **Reject Note** to decline it, or **Cancel** to close without changes.

![Review Indent Request](/img/store/indent/indent-requests-view.png)
