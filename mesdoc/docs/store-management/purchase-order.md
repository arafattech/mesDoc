# PI Order

Track and manage Purchase Indent (PI) orders raised against approved indents.

## PI Order List
To view all purchase orders:
1. Navigate to **Store Management > PI Order**.
2. Use the **Approved**, **Canceled**, and **Pending** tabs to filter records by status.
3. The list shows PI No, SPR No (Indent reference), Supplier, PI Date, Total Amount, Status, and Complete progress.

![Purchase Order List](/img/store/purchase-order/purchase-order.png)

## View Purchase Order Details
To view details of a specific PO:
1. Click the **View Details** icon in the Actions column of a row.
2. Review the PO No, PO Date, Status, SPR List, Supplier, Note, and the ordered Items with Qty, Unit Price, and Delivery Date.

![Purchase Order View](/img/store/purchase-order/purchase-order-view.png)
