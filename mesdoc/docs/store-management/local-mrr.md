# Local MRR

This module lets you record and track Material Receipt Records (MRR) for goods received locally against Purchase Orders (PO).

## View Local MRR List
To view the Local MRR list:
1. Navigate to **Store Management > Local MRR**.
2. Use the **Partial**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled** tabs to filter records by status.
3. Use **Search** to find a record, or click a column filter icon to filter by that column.
4. Click a row to load its **MRR Log** (received items) in the panel on the right.
5. Click the **Open PDF** icon in Actions to download the MRR as a PDF.

![Local MRR List](/img/store/local-mrr/local-mrr-list.png)

## View MRR Log
To view the products received against an MRR:
1. Click on any row in the Local MRR list.
2. The **MRR Log** panel on the right shows Product, Required Qty, Received Qty, Receiving Qty, Date, and Received By.

![MRR Log](/img/store/local-mrr/local-mrr-log.png)

## Create a New Local MRR
To create a new Material Receipt Record:
1. Click the **New** button on the Local MRR page.
2. Fill in **MRR No** (auto-generated), **Receipt Date**, **PI/Indent List**, and **Supplier Name**.
3. Optionally add a **General Remark**.
4. Add items to receive in the **Items** table.
5. Click **Submit** to save the record, or **Cancel** to discard.

![Create Local MRR](/img/store/local-mrr/local-mrr-add.png)
