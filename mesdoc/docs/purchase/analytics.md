# Purchase Analytics

The **Purchase Analytics** dashboard provides a comprehensive visual overview and analytical breakdown of all purchase-related activities. It helps management, procurement officers, and store managers track purchase requisitions, orders, stock values, and supplier performance efficiently.

## Accessing Purchase Analytics
To view the analytics dashboard:
1. Open the main menu on the left sidebar.
2. Navigate to **Purchase Management** > **Purchase Analytics**.
3. The page will load the key metrics, graphs, tables, and filters.

![Purchase Analytics Dashboard](/img/purchase/analytics/analytices.png)

---

## Key Features & Visual Elements

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPI Cards)
At the very top of the dashboard, four main metric cards present high-level summarized data:
*   **Total P/O**: The total number of Purchase Orders generated.
*   **Total SPR**: The total number of Store Purchase Requisitions/Requests submitted.
*   **Orders**: The total volume of items/orders processed.
*   **Stock Value**: The total monetary value of current warehouse stock.

### 2. Graphical Reports & Charts
*   **Recent – Activity (Bar Chart)**: A month-over-month bar graph depicting purchase volumes and activity trends (from August through January).
*   **Department Distribution (Donut Chart)**: A percentage breakdown of purchase requests distributed across different departments (e.g., Store, IT Support, Research & Development, Production).

### 3. Data Tables & Lists
*   **Latest PO Table**: A detailed list of the most recently created Purchase Orders showing:
    *   **Item Code**: The unique code of the purchased item.
    *   **Department Name**: The department requesting the item.
    *   **Supplier Name**: The vendor fulfilling the order.
    *   **Amount**: The financial value of the order.
    *   *Features*: Includes inline search, column-specific filtering, sorting options, and custom column visibility settings.
*   **Top Suppliers List**: A ranking of the top three suppliers based on total transaction values, displaying the supplier name, ID, and total amount.

---

## Filtering Analytics Data
To filter and refine the dashboard metrics:
1. Click the **Filter** button in the top right corner of the page.
2. A slide-out/modal dialog will appear with the following options:
   *   **Date Range**: Select a custom start and end date to analyze specific periods.
   *   **Department**: Choose a specific department from the dropdown list (e.g., Human Resources, Store, Production, Inventory Control, Logistics).
   *   **Supplier**: Filter results for a particular vendor from the supplier list.
3. Click **Apply** to refresh the metrics, **Reset** to clear the selected criteria, or **Cancel** to close the window without changes.
