# Purchase Order (PO)

The **Purchase Order (PO)** module is the final stage of the procurement workflow. A Purchase Order is a legally binding document sent to a supplier, indicating the types, quantities, and agreed prices for products or services.

---

## 1. Purchase Orders List Page
To view all generated Purchase Orders, navigate to **Purchase Management** > **Purchase Order** from the main menu. 

The Purchase Orders table contains the following columns:
*   **PO Code**: The unique code assigned to the Purchase Order (e.g., `PO-000018`).
*   **Supplier Name**: The supplier/vendor contracted for the purchase.
*   **Date**: The date the PO was generated.
*   **Amount**: The total cost of the order (currency-adjusted).
*   **Requisition Status**: Current state of the PO in the approval lifecycle (e.g., *Draft*, *Checked*, *Approved*, *Rejected*).
*   **Actions**:
    *   **View Details (Eye Icon)**: View the item breakdown, shipping address, and approval history.

---

## 2. Viewing Purchase Order Details
Clicking the view action button on a Purchase Order row opens a details drawer containing:
*   **Vendor and Delivery Info**: Displays vendor contact info alongside delivery terms and shipping/billing destinations.
*   **Item Breakdown Grid**: An itemized list showing:
    *   Item Name and Code
    *   Ordered Quantity
    *   Unit Price and Subtotal
    *   Applicable Taxes and Discounts
*   **Total Summary**: Calculated sum of Subtotal, Tax/VAT, and Grand Total.
*   **Approval & Status Timeline**: Tracks the dates and names of users who drafted, checked, and approved the order.

![Purchase Order View](/img/purchase/purchase-orders/purchase-orders-view.png)

---

## 3. Creating/Generating a Purchase Order
While POs are typically auto-generated by selecting the winning bid in the **Comparative Study** module:
1. They can also be created manually by clicking the **New** or **Add New** button on the PO page (if permissions allow).
2. The user selects the supplier, links the approved requisition, adds items, specifies delivery schedules, and enters financial details.
3. Once submitted, the PO moves to a **Draft** or **Pending Approval** status, awaiting authorization from procurement heads.
